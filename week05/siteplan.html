Website planning document

Taste of Ghana

File and Folder Names

Project files use lowercase, descriptive names with no spaces. Pages include index.html, dishes.html, recipes.html, regions.html, contact.html, references.html and siteplan.html. The site styles, scripts and images are organized in the lowercase styles/, scripts/ and images/ folders. This standalone plan keeps its styles and year display in siteplan.html.

Site Name

Taste of Ghana is a friendly guide to the meals and food traditions I grew up with. The name is short, easy to remember and tells visitors exactly what the site celebrates.

Site Purpose

The site introduces popular Ghanaian dishes, shares approachable recipes and ingredient amounts, and connects foods with the regions and communities where they are enjoyed. Visitors can search and filter the photo gallery, save favorite dishes, print recipes and suggest an idea.

Scenarios

  • How do I make jollof rice for a family dinner?
  • Which Ghanaian dishes are associated with the coast or the north?
  • Can I adjust a recipe for a different number of servings and print it?

Color Schema

  • #173C30 – Header and footer; a deep forest green.
  • #C3A36B – Fine accents and buttons; a muted champagne gold.
  • #A45B48 – Occasional warm highlights, echoing Ghana's red.
  • #F6F2E9 – Soft ivory canvas for content.
  • #24231F – Main text color for readable content.

Typography

Cormorant Garamond — used for page titles and dish headings.

Manrope — used for body text, forms and navigation.
